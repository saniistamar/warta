# Student guide — your warta sitting

> What happens when you run warta, what you decide, and what you walk
> away with. Read this once. About five minutes.

## 1. What this is

warta is a guided workflow for reporting a story. You bring a topic —
something you keep hearing about, like "jakarta sinking city". The agent
does the heavy lifting: searching, sorting, drafting options, keeping
records. You make the calls a journalist makes. It runs in Goose, on
your laptop, in one sitting of about 2-3 hours.

## 2. What you decide

Five decisions are yours. Everything else, the agent carries.

| Stage | You decide |
| --- | --- |
| Landscape | your topic, and where to steer the scan |
| Story pick | one story from the shortlist, and its "why now" in your own words |
| Evidence | which source is best for each claim, and what to do with risky ones |
| Angle pick | the lens of the story, and who it is for |
| Materialize | the medium, and the final sign-off |

The workflow will not skip ahead of you. That is on purpose.

## 3. What you walk away with

A folder under `runs/<your-story>/` holding:

- **The story brief** — your story in one page: angle, audience,
  why-now, and every claim tied to a logged source.
- **The claim log** — each claim, its best source, and its status. Your
  evidence trail.
- **The landscape and story files** — the path you took, kept for
  reference.
- **The storyboard page** — your board as a picture: open
  `storyboard.html` in any browser. It renders straight from the
  board, so it always matches it.

A writer — or a drafting agent — can pick the brief up cold and start
work. That is the test of the format.

## 4. If something breaks

- No search results at the very start means search is not set up. Stop
  and see the Troubleshooting section of the README.
- If Goose closes mid-session, reopen it in the same folder and say
  "resume warta". It picks up where you stopped.
- When a claim looks shaky, flag it and say what you want to do. Owning
  a failed check is part of the work, not a failure of it.
